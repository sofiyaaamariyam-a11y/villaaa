Ukulhas is located in Alif Alif Atoll, Maldives. Guests typically travel from Velana International Airport to the island by scheduled speedboat or other available transfer arrangements.

Transfer schedules, prices and journey times may vary. Contact us before your arrival so your journey can be planned.
