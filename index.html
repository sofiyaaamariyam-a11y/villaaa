Let the island welcome you.

Ukulhas is located in Alif Alif Atoll, North Ari. Travellers generally reach the island from Malé by scheduled speedboat or public ferry.

Transfer schedules and prices can change. Contact the villa to confirm the best transfer option, departure time and pickup arrangements before travelling.
